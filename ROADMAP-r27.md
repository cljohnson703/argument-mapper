## Approved, not yet built

* **Davidson's trick, two more steps.** Existential introduction on objects ("Bob loves Mary; so Bob loves someone"), and dropping a place or instrument phrase ("Bob ran in the park; so Bob ran"; "Bob buttered the toast with a knife; so Bob buttered the toast"). The user: "So long as it continues to respect the limits of Davidson's trick." Not after "almost", "allegedly", "hardly", "fake" or "former", nothing under "not", and no idioms ("fell in love", "ran into Mary").
* **"Able to fly and swim".** "Everyone who is able to fly and swim is happy; Poe can fly, and Poe can swim; so Poe is happy" says it needs "Poe is a swim": "swim" is read as a noun. The user: "ok" to fixing it in a later release.
